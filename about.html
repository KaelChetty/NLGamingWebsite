About Us

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena is a gaming and entertainment destination located in Johannesburg, South Africa.

Our goal is to provide an exciting environment where gamers, friends and families can enjoy a variety of interactive gaming experiences.

From virtual reality and racing simulators to competitive esports experiences, there is something for different types of players.

Our Mission

Our mission is to create an enjoyable and welcoming gaming environment where people can connect, compete and experience entertainment in a different way.

What We Offer

Next Level Gaming & Esports Arena offers a range of gaming and entertainment experiences including virtual reality, racing simulators, esports training, gaming packages, birthday experiences and escape room challenges.
